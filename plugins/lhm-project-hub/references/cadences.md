# Cadences & SLAs

| Rhythm | What | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Same business day | Reply to client emails | Whoever received it |
| Within 24h of a meeting | Meeting-wrap email to client (client-meeting-email) | Meeting attendee |
| Every 48h | Chase outstanding client approvals (ad copy, LP copy, designs) | KP |
| 3 days after expected first charge | GoCardless payment check | Josephine |
| Weekly (every 7 days) during first 30 days | Client progress update (completed / next / approvals needed) | KP |
| Day 14–16 of onboarding | Mid-point check-in (internal + client) | KP + Michael |
| Day 30 of onboarding | Close-out: verify all deliverables, transition to ongoing rhythm | KP |
| Monthly | Monthly wrap per client (monthly-review, wrap mode) | KP |
| Monthly | Client meeting (or recap email if no meeting) | Michael + KP |
| Monthly | Internal account review (monthly-review, internal mode) | Michael |
| Quarterly | Strategy review + next 3/6-month campaign plan | Michael |

Escalation rules:
- Organic traffic down ≥20% month-on-month → flag to Michael immediately.
- GoCardless first payment failed → notify Michael, halt Xero setup until resolved.
- No client update sent in >7 days on an active project → pm-orchestrator flags it.
