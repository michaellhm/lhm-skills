## 1. Welcome & Access Request

**Subject:** Welcome to LHM — let's get your accounts connected

Hi [First name],

Welcome to Local Health Marketing. We're glad to have [Client] on board and keen to get moving.

I'm Kristalyn (most people call me KP), and I'll be your main point of contact from here. Any questions about your account, day-to-day updates, or just wanting to know where things are at, come straight to me.

Before we can start work, we need access to a few of your platforms. Here's what we need and how to grant each one:

- **Google Analytics (GA4)** — Add lhmanalytics@gmail.com as an Admin user under Property Access Management.
- **Google Tag Manager (GTM)** — Add lhmanalytics@gmail.com as an Admin under Container Access.
- **Google Business Profile (GBP)** — Add lhmanalytics@gmail.com as an Owner under Business Profile Settings > People and Access.
- **Google Ads** — Send us your 10-digit Customer ID (top right of your Google Ads account) so we can link it into our management account.
- **Website CMS** — Add support@localhealthmarketing.com.au as an Admin user.

If any of this feels unfamiliar or you're not sure who holds the keys to these accounts, no problem at all. Reply to this email and we'll set up a short call to walk through it together, screen to screen.

Looking forward to getting started.

Thanks,
KP
Local Health Marketing
