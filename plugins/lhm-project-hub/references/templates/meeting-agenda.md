# Monthly Client Meeting Agenda

Timed agenda for the recurring monthly client meeting (cadences.md).

## Pre-meeting self-check (internal, before every meeting)

- Any open issues on this account we haven't flagged yet?
- Are we waiting on any content, approvals, or access from the client?

Resolve or at least be ready to speak to both before the meeting starts.

## Agenda (30 minutes)

1. **Welcome** (2m)
   Quick warm-up, how's business been, any changes on their end.

2. **Project updates & deliverables** (10m)
   Walk through what's been done since last meeting, project by project.

3. **Last meeting's action items** (3m)
   Review what was agreed last time, confirm status on each.

4. **"How did you do last month?"** (5m)
   Patients seen, new patients, and how many were attributable to Google.
   Get this from the client directly, it's the real-world number that
   validates (or challenges) what the dashboards say.

5. **Analytics & performance review** (5m)
   Walk through the numbers: traffic, leads, ad performance, Maps visibility.

6. **Goals & next steps** (3m)
   Confirm what we're focused on next and any goals that need adjusting.

7. **AOB (any other business)** (1m)
   Anything not already covered.

8. **Wrap-up** (1m)
   Recap agreed actions and owners, confirm the date of the next meeting.
