# Service Kickoff Email Templates

One parameterised email per service. Parameters: `[Client]`, `[First name]`,
`[milestone date]`. All route the client to KP as their contact.

## Website

**Subject:** Your new website project is underway

Hi [First name],

We're kicking off your website project. Next up, our team will map out your site structure and start on design direction based on everything you shared with us.

To keep things moving, we'll need your logo files, any existing brand guidelines, and photos you'd like used on the site. If you don't have professional photos, let us know and we'll talk through options.

First milestone: sitemap and design concepts ready for your review by [milestone date].

Kristalyn (KP) is your contact for anything website-related, questions, feedback, or content. Talk soon.

Thanks,
The LHM Team
