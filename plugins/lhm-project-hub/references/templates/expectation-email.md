Now that we're underway, I wanted to send a short note on how our engagement works, so there are no surprises down the track.

**What's included in your package**

Your Tier 1 package covers Google Ads management and Google Maps (Google Business Profile) management, at $1,750/month. That includes campaign strategy, ongoing optimisation, ad copy and asset updates, and local SEO work on your Maps listing. If you've signed on for any add-ons, those are covered too, on the terms in your agreement.

**What's not included**

Some requests fall outside this scope, for example:

- New features or pages on your website
- Photography or videography
- Setting up or managing third-party tools outside Google Ads and GBP

These aren't off the table. We're happy to help. We'll just scope and quote the work separately before starting, so you always know what you're paying for.

**How fast we respond**

We reply to emails the same business day. For anything urgent, flag it as urgent in the subject line and we'll prioritise it.

**How we keep you in the loop**

We run a monthly check-in meeting to go through performance, what's coming next, and any decisions we need from you. If a meeting isn't possible that month, we'll send a recap email instead so you're never left wondering what's happening.

If anything here isn't clear, or you want to talk through what's covered, just reply and we'll sort it out.
