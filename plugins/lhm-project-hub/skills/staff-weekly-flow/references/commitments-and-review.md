# Commitments chosen by the person, reviewed with them

Michael confirmed this operating model on 28 September 2026. Use it for every staff-flow person,
including Michael. The brief and BasicOps identify work to consider; they do not prove consent to
a weekly commitment. Coach for clarity, choice, delivery and early communication, not task counts.

## One shared record per person and week

Resolve the canonical person's name/folder from their verified People profile. Reuse
`22 People/<Person>/YYYY-Www — Weekly Flow.md` in **LHM Knowledge**. Do not create a parallel
commitment database, duplicate person alias, or a second weekly note. Confirm the planning week
and timezone; use the current portfolio's Melbourne ISO week when planning from its Monday brief,
while showing deadlines and availability in the person's verified timezone. Link the source brief.

Save the explicitly agreed work commitments here, so Michael can review what each person chose
and what happened. Save work outcomes, decisions, blockers and handoffs only. Do not save private
reasons, health/family details, founder reflections or another person's personal assessment in the
shared record. Use a neutral capacity summary when needed, such as "two delivery blocks available".

Each commitment has a stable ID within the note (C01, C02...), outcome, linked source task/project,
completion evidence/check, chosen timing or next checkpoint, dependency/next recipient, explicit
acceptance and result. Keep the same ID as its state changes. The note is a compact commitment
record, not a copy of every BasicOps field or the person's full board.

Use these sections in the existing weekly note:

### Last week: commitments and results

| Prior commitment/link | What happened | Evidence/confidence | What we learned or need next |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

### This week: confirmed commitments

| ID | Outcome and source | Done means | Chosen timing/checkpoint | Dependency and handoff | Acceptance | Result/evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Record acceptance as "Confirmed by <person>, <date/time/timezone>" only after their clear answer.
Proposals belong under **Candidates awaiting a decision**, not in this table. Timing may be an
agreed checkpoint or "not yet agreed"; never invent a deadline. A person's planning date does not
alter an existing client promise or BasicOps due date.

### Not committed this week

| Candidate/source | Person's decision | Dependency or client consequence | Next owner/checkpoint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Possible decisions: decline this week, defer, awaiting input, ask for help, propose delegation,
renegotiate, or deliberately drop. Do not demand a personal justification. A decision not to take
work is valid planning information, not failure. If a client promise or another person's work is
affected, identify who must renegotiate/reassign it; do not silently cancel a commitment externally.

### Dated progress and decisions

Append who reported/confirmed the update, time, commitment ID, changed state, evidence and next
handoff. Keep "reported complete", "verified complete", "awaiting review", "in progress",
"blocked", "not started", "renegotiated" and "withdrawn by agreement" distinct. A completion
claim is attributed until supported by the deliverable, current task discussion or human acceptance.
Do not turn saved note text into an unverified BasicOps completion or sent message.

## Monday conversation

1. Read the previous confirmed weekly file, current file if any, current shared brief/corrections,
   and relevant live tasks. If no previous confirmed record exists, say so. Discuss reported
   carryover without retroactively manufacturing last week's promises.
2. Review each previous commitment with the person: what was achieved, what is awaiting review,
   what changed, and what would help? Start from evidence and ask one useful question at a time.
   Avoid blame, scorekeeping, surveillance language or treating task volume as performance.
3. Record the result against the original commitment in last week's note, preserving its history.
   If work continues, link to that ID from a proposed new-week candidate; carryover is not automatic.
4. Present a small set of relevant this-week candidates, with the outcome, why it matters, deadline
   if real, dependencies and rough effort uncertainty. Ask what the person can and wants to commit
   to. They may change order, propose another approach, reduce scope, ask for help or say no.
5. Separate accepted work, proposals, optional work and non-commitments. Resolve client/dependency
   consequences through the appropriate owner. Never treat the whole brief, a manager's request,
   assigned task, silence, "maybe" or a default recommendation as the person's acceptance.
6. Reflect back the exact selected outcomes and checks. Their explicit confirmation authorises
   saving that agreed plan and its shared work commitments; do not ask a second generic save question.
   Before confirmation, save only a clearly labelled draft when they explicitly request one.
7. When requested, apply exact board changes through `basicops-task-manager` and read back each
   result. Confirmation to save an Obsidian plan alone does not authorise every possible task mutation.
8. Finish with what they chose, what they did not choose, help/handoffs needed, the first next action
   and a link to the saved record. Offer a short team update draft if useful; sending needs consent.

## Progress during the week

Use the confirmed file for daily selection. Save clear, relevant progress feedback as an attributed
dated update; ask only when its meaning or consequence is unclear. Do not silently change the
original acceptance, increase scope or roll dates forward. A changed commitment needs the person's
agreement and an explicit disposition of the earlier one. Append shared project corrections to
the weekly portfolio note as well, linking the person's record; do not copy private capacity detail.

Re-read before each write, merge only scoped updates, deduplicate by source and commitment ID,
preserve others' entries, and read back. Report a failed write as pending with exact content. An
interrupted session resumes from saved decisions without re-asking already confirmed choices.

## Michael's team review

When Michael asks to review team commitments, read only the authorised shared weekly records and
relevant source tasks. Summarise chosen outcomes, verified/reported results, help needed and
unaccepted work with consequences. Link to each person's record. Missing records mean
"no confirmed plan recorded", not "the person did nothing". Do not draft or confirm commitments
on a person's behalf, infer private reasons or rank people by completion counts. Use the findings
to prepare supportive coaching questions and unblock decisions. No extra reminder or monitoring
automation is created by this skill.

## Acceptance scenarios

- "I'll commit to C01 and C03; not C02": save only the selected commitments after exact confirmation;
  record C02 as not committed with any known dependency and next owner, without invented blame.
- "Maybe Friday": leave timing proposed; do not convert it to acceptance or a new client deadline.
- Next Monday, C01 is complete and C03 stalled: verify/attribute both, record results in the original
  note, ask what changed and whether C03 should be recommitted; do not silently carry it forward.
- Michael asks what Aiya chose: return her shared confirmed plan and evidence, not private capacity
  details or the entire assigned backlog. Missing plan is explicitly unknown.
- "Run my staff flow" from Michael: start this same loop without requiring his business review.
- Two updates or a retry: stable IDs, merged history and source dedup prevent duplicate commitments.
