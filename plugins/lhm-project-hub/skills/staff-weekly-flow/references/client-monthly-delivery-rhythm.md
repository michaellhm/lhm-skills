# Client monthly delivery rhythm

Read the canonical vault note `60 Knowledge/Client Monthly Delivery Rhythm.md` during weekly
planning for Michael, Jaimee or Kristalyn.

## Role selection

- Michael receives the clients whose `Ads week` matches the active four-week operating week, plus
  every actual client meeting from the previous seven days whose commitments lack a verified
  follow-up review.
- Jaimee receives the clients whose `SEO week` matches. Carry forward relevant meeting and Ads
  findings; skip clients explicitly recorded as Ads-only and flag an unconfirmed SEO scope.
- Kristalyn receives the clients whose `Readiness week` matches. Check promised work, incomplete
  handoffs, missing client inputs, pushback and preparation for the next client meeting/touchpoint.
- Aiya and Josephine receive cycle work only when a real action or handoff names them; the matrix is
  not a reason to invent routine work for them.

## Evidence and red flags

Do not count a reminder card, due date, task status or absence of complaints as completion. Require
a dated evidence link and result/next handoff in the note's completion ledger.

- current pass without evidence: `due/missed`;
- previous monthly pass still missing when the next becomes due: visible red flag;
- two consecutive missing monthly passes: high-priority red flag for the owner and Michael;
- meeting older than seven days without commitment review: overdue meeting follow-up;
- completed work without its required handoff: broken handoff, not complete flow.

When a pass cannot fit, record `reschedule`, `delegate` or `communicate` in the proposed weekly file.
Never silently drop it. Prepare ledger updates and BasicOps mutations separately and apply only
after the relevant approval.

## BasicOps boundary

The Week 1–4 BasicOps reminder parents/subtasks are migration evidence during the first live cycle.
Read real BasicOps actions and exceptions, but do not create new generic meeting-review or Ads-week
cards. Never complete/archive the legacy cards without an explicit reviewed migration decision.
