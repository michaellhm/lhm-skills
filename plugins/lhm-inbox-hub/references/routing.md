Canonical copy of this file lives in the Obsidian vault at `LHM/Operations/email-routing.md`. This file is the seed, built from a 30-day analysis of sent mail (Sept 2026). When the vault copy exists, read the vault copy and treat this one as a fallback. Per-client contact detail belongs in each client's `client_profile.md` under an `## Email` heading, not here.

## Tiers

**Tier 1, Michael replies (goes in the brief with a draft)**
- Any client contact asking a strategy, performance, spend, technical, or "what's happening" question.
- Any partner (Shane, Marlon, Michelle at Voyager, Yoni, Simon, Sam at Bizwisdom, Mac Lackey) unless it is pure scheduling.
- Prospects and referrals (new enquiries, proposals, anyone introduced by a partner).
- Vendor threads Michael started (Shaun at Adpulse, GHL support, Search Atlas).
- Anything from Kristalyn that asks for a decision or acceptance.

**Tier 2, Josephine handles (brief notes it; no draft unless she has not acknowledged within 1 business day)**
- Meeting reschedules and Google Meet links.
- Availability requests and booking-link replies.
- Invoices, Xero, GoCardless, card updates, subscription cancellations, offboarding admin.
- NAP / address / phone confirmations for local SEO.
- Chasing pending Briefly article tasks.
- Google Ads billing how-tos.
- Her holding template for anything technical is: "Thanks for flagging this. Michael will look into this and get back to you with an update shortly." When that template has gone out, the thread is Tier 1 for Michael and the clock is running.

**Tier 3, team runs it (label FYI, no draft)**
- Threads where Kristalyn, Jaimee, or Aiya is already the last LHM sender and the client is replying to them.
- support@ threads answered by Josephine or Kristalyn.
- Internal "Meeting wrap" digests Josephine sends from Michael's mailbox.

**Tier 4, noise (skip, do not list)**
- Subscriptions, newsletters, Pabbly, Loom, Asana, Search Console notices, Drive/Docs/Dropbox share notifications, Cliniko/Twilio auto-mails, BasicOps notification emails, Marmot lead-form relays, "Sitemap rollout" self-notes.
