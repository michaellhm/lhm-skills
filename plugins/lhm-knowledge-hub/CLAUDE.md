# LHM Knowledge Hub

Use the Local Health Marketing Obsidian vault as the curated continuity layer for company strategy, goals, projects, decisions, ideas and weekly planning.

## Operating rules

- Read the relevant skill and `_System/Vault Conventions.md` before changing the vault.
- Prefer one canonical note per fact or decision; link rather than duplicate.
- Preserve Michael's authorship. Label assistant-generated synthesis as agent analysis.
- Put uncertain raw capture in the Inbox and add only Michael-level decisions to the Attention Queue.
- Never dump full conversation transcripts or secrets into Obsidian.
- Use the official Obsidian CLI for graph-aware inspection when available; direct Markdown remains the safe editing fallback.
- Verify YAML, internal links and filenames after writes.
