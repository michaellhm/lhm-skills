# Diagnosis and status

## Evidence ledger

For each source, record property/profile ID, scope, window, retrieval date, definition, completeness and source link. Label unavailable access and stale snapshots. Restrict saved data to business aggregates; remove patient details and booking confirmation tokens. Do not copy client data into the skill repository.

- **Business direction:** current Goals and meeting decisions; distinguish confirmed decisions from ideas. Resolve a meeting question from Fathom or the saved wrap before asking a human. Preserve effective dates and sources.
- **GA4:** establish the actual booking/lead event, tracking continuity and landing-page/device/channel breakdowns. An event count is not unique patients or attended appointments. A campaign labelled with a suburb identifies acquisition, not necessarily booked clinic. Inspect booking-confirmation paths without submitting bookings or retaining identifying query strings. Match event/session denominators; event-per-session yield is not a deduplicated conversion rate.
- **Search Console:** compare clicks, impressions, CTR and average position by relevant query/page, country and device where useful. Keep tagged and clean URLs distinct. Average position is not a fixed rank or Maps grid result. Query rows can exclude anonymised queries or hit extraction caps; do not sum capped results as complete totals or treat absent rows as zero. A fall in clicks with rising impressions and mixed positions does not establish a ranking collapse.
- **Maps/GBP:** use each location's actual focus keywords and comparable grid centre, size, radius and dates. No composite across incompatible scans. Existing baseline does not prove later improvement. Read-only access does not authorise paid scans.
- **Google Ads:** prefer actual converting search terms with action definitions, period, volume and cost. Keywords and actual queries differ. Linked GA4 Ads-query booking events are a useful explicitly labelled fallback, not Ads-native attributed conversions, CPA or proof of organic profitability. Mark brand/non-brand and service/location intent.
- **Delivery:** compare previous recommendations against dated live or artefact evidence. Drafted, scheduled, published and verified are different. Inspect historical directory flags before claiming they still apply. Reconcile website-rebuild plans so work is not duplicated.

Use consistent periods; if a shorter month, seasonality or small count changes the interpretation, say so. Check longer history or a comparable prior-year period when available, without claiming causal seasonality from a short series. Reports with different attribution methods must remain separate until reconciled.

## Investigation to action

For an outcome decline, test whether fewer visits, lower recorded booking yield, tracking changes or a combination explains the observations. Drill into the location, channel, device, page and queries implicated. State the evidence for and against the leading explanation. A descriptive decomposition is not causal proof.

Recommend the smallest supported next action. Give exact URLs/fields and draft wording where evidence permits. If a critical step requires unavailable dashboard access, state what the AI already checked and the precise remaining check. Do not assign vague “investigate SEO” work or make the team repeat completed analysis. Do not conclude appointment availability from static booking-widget placeholders.

## Traffic lights

Use approved client thresholds when present, with the rule and evidence. Otherwise use labelled qualitative pilot assessments; do not invent universal percentage cut-offs or quietly convert provisional targets into commitments.

| Dimension | Red | Amber | Green | Unknown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Performance | Material verified failure or severe sustained underperformance against the goal | Meaningful decline, mixed signals or issue needing attention | Sufficient evidence performance is on track | Insufficient, stale or unreconciled measurement |
| Programme delivery | Required work overdue or a critical blocker | Open work at risk, with a viable next action | Required work on track with evidence | Stage or completion disputed/unverified |

Always attach the reason, evidence period and confidence. Do not colour unknown performance green because a phase is complete. Keep performance and delivery separate. Do not average local colours into a misleading client-wide score. The summary should surface the highest-priority action and coverage gaps.

## Phase-aware work

- **Month 0:** list the actual unfinished onboarding tasks and exit evidence. Typical areas are profile fields/verification, citations, architecture, baseline and content planning, but derive the queue from that client's checklist. Verify uncertain items separately; reuse completed diagnostics.
- **Month 1:** record service-page changes, dates, technical/indexing checks and initial results against baseline. Queue supporting content based on actual needs.
- **Month 2:** assess service-page outcomes and the diagnostic direction; choose appropriate supporting FAQ/local content, recording publication and results separately. Prepare the link queue.
- **Month 3:** review the whole cycle, completed pages, acquired links, outcome/keyword trends, carry-over and proposed next services. Do not credit a link outreach draft as an acquired link.

Monthly opportunity discovery complements these stages. Align content and PR with client goals and offered services. Quarterly review reconsiders goals, the Ads-to-organic opportunity set and next-cycle service priorities. Updating a page alone is not a guaranteed ranking benefit.
