# Chief of Staff source handoff
Create one per-run registration request for the exact approved Drive, Fathom and destination identifiers, then submit the matching closed manifest with `/opt/data/profiles/lhm_brain/bin/source-dispatch submit`. Poll `source-dispatch status <run_id>`. Do not draft, deliver or mark `all_required` complete unless status reports two verified retrieval receipts and a verified delivery receipt. On a typed blocker, preserve the parent and let the executable CTO route own recovery.
