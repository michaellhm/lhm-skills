# Accepted editorial and reliability rules

Historical examples are regression cases, never current client facts.

- Include each issue once, with the latest evidence, next actor and useful
  action/question. Do not repeat it in actions, questions, progress and waits.
- A stale board label does not outweigh a dated ready-for-review discussion.
  Distinguish the implementer, current reviewer and later client handoff.
  Approval does not prove publication or that a client email was sent.
- Search issue-specific internal notifications as well as client-domain email.
  Read the newest substantive reply before assigning the next action.
- Keep staging/mockups distinct from production. Verify exact dates from the
  latest relevant source and check attendee leave against the meeting date.
- Omit excluded topics from mixed threads entirely. Preserve the deterministic
  excluded-topic guard and all delivery receipts.
- A research budget cutoff is not successful delivery. Save partial evidence,
  resume within the bounded retry policy and require recipient delivery events.
- Cover all three research sources early and reserve time for reconciliation.
  A failed source may be disclosed as a limitation after one retry; an
  unattempted source must not be labelled checked or unavailable.
