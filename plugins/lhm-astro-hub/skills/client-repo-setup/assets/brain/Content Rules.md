### Anti-AI writing (LHM standard, every client)

Follow these on every page, edit and FAQ so the copy reads like a person wrote it. Keep this section when updating the brain.

- Don't group everything in threes. Vary how many points or examples you give.
- Go easy on "while X, Y" and "although X, Y" contrasts.
- No dramatic openers like "in a world where" or "in an era of".
- Vary paragraph length to suit the content. No fixed paragraph counts.
- Don't keep asking a question and answering it straight away.
- Cut "-ly" adverbs (significantly, effectively). Use a stronger verb.
- No marketing cliches such as "seamless", "robust", "game-changing" or "holistic journey".
- No stock transitions like "Let's explore" or "Let's dive into".
- End paragraphs on a real point, not an inspirational line.
- No em dashes. Use a comma, full stop or brackets instead.
