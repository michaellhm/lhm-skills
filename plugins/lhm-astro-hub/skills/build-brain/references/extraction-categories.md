# Extraction categories

What to pull from the client's Fathom calls, the approved prototype copy and `brain/Brand.md`, and which brain note each item lands in. If something is not stated, leave the note's `> gap:` line in place. Never fill it from memory or from what is typical for the industry.

Every fact written to a note adds its source (call title and date, "prototype copy", or "Brand.md") to that note's `sources`.

## Business foundation -> `Profile.md`

- Business name and industry (top of the note)
- Mission statement, explicit or clearly implied -> Mission
- North star, tagline or big promise -> Vision
- Core values, stated directly or shown in how they talk -> Values
- Founder's name, background, why they started, business origin -> Founder story
- What makes them different, proof points (certifications, partnerships, methodology, authority figures), treatment or service philosophy -> Positioning

## Product and service details -> `Services.md`

- Core offerings -> Services offered
- Tiers, pricing philosophy, prices only where the client stated them, booking and delivery model -> Pricing and booking
- Guarantees or promises, what they do not treat or offer, referral requirements -> Conditions and exclusions

## People -> `Staff.md`

- Who works there, role, qualifications, areas of focus, how the client wants each person introduced. Names and credentials exactly as the client gave them.

## Solutions -> `What We Do.md`

- How the business addresses each customer concern, the approach and process a patient or customer goes through, and the outcomes the client is willing to claim (only as they stated them).

## Target audience and customer psychology -> `Who We Serve.md`

- Primary and secondary audiences, who they do not target
- Where customers are in their journey when they arrive
- Concerns, fears, emotional state, pain points, common objections and hesitations
- Social proof themes the client mentioned (testimonial themes, story categories to collect), not invented testimonials

## Voice and writing preferences -> `Content Rules.md`

- Voice and tone preferences, personality traits as the client describes themselves
- Words and phrases the client uses often
- Words and phrases they avoid or dislike, and the alternatives they prefer
- How they want people to talk to customers
- Compliance limits that apply to copy (for example AHPRA)

## Visual identity -> `Brand.md`

Already written by `astro-prototype`. Read it, do not rewrite it. Only append to `sources` when a call adds a verified detail, and say so in the draft.

## Out of scope for the brain

Sales scripts, objection-handling scripts and discovery questions belong to the client's sales process, not the website brain. Skip them.
