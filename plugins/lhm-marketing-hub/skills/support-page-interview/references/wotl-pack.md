## Bundled Children-page structure

This snapshot lets the interview run without browsing:
- For parents/carers: understanding worry, big feelings and daily disruption.
- **When ordinary days are becoming difficult:** worries, overwhelming feelings, school/friendships and changes at home/school.
- **What you and your child could work towards:** understanding/expressing feelings, responding to worry, everyday challenges and helping parents support their child. Goals vary; no guaranteed outcome.
- **Understanding the experience behind the behaviour:** both child and parent perspectives, age-appropriate work and support between appointments.
- **Making sense of a continuing concern:** seek professional advice when persistent/intense difficulties affect daily participation; examples are not a diagnostic checklist.
- **Questions parents often ask:** need for support, first appointment, parent involvement, GP referrals, therapy versus assessment and session numbers. These are draft topics for Nicole to correct, not clinic-approved answers.
- **Information for parents and carers:** clinician-recommended resources.
- **Tell us what you have noticed:** enquiry and practical next steps.

Keep this problem → realistic goals → understanding/approach → questions → next step flow across other support pages. The wording and clinical content must come from the participant, not this snapshot.

## Practice context

Wellness On The Lane is a group of independent businesses with clinicians consulting from rooms, not one employed clinical team. Do not imply every clinician treats every concern, offers each service, or works at every location. Published prototype navigation is a discussion starting point, not a verified service roster. Nicole can confirm her own approach and identify who should answer wider practice questions. Crestina coordinates administrative facts and clinician inputs; Michael prepares website copy for review.

The proposed page list combines audience, concern and service pages. Let Nicole identify which she can answer herself and which should go to another clinician. Supervision needs a professional audience, not the Children-page patient framing. Do not infer fees, rebates, ages, consent arrangements, diagnoses, availability or methods. No sign-in to LHM tools, patient systems or website editing is required.
