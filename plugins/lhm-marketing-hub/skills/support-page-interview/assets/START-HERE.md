# Nicole — website content interviews

This folder helps you give Michael the clinical content for the Wellness On The Lane website. You do not need to write polished website copy.

1. Unzip the workspace onto your computer, somewhere you can find again.
2. Open a desktop AI app that can read and write files in a folder. Give it access to this unzipped folder. A browser-only chat without local file access cannot save your progress automatically.
3. Paste this message:

> Read support-page-interview/SKILL.md and follow it using the Wellness On The Lane pack. My interview files are in answers/support-page-interview. Start with the Children page, or resume my saved progress if I have already started. Ask one question at a time, and save after each answer. Tell me if you cannot read or write these files.

You can type or talk using your app’s voice input. Rough answers are fine. Please describe general patterns in your work, not identifiable patients. Your files stay on your computer until you send them, but your AI app processes what you type or dictate under its own settings.

Useful things to say:
- “Pause here and save my progress.”
- “Continue where we stopped.”
- “That topic needs another clinician.”
- “Let me correct that answer.”
- “Show me the summary for this page.”
- “Finish this page.”
- “Export my completed and partial pages into a ZIP.”

There is a proposed list of 14 pages. They are starting points from the website navigation, not a requirement to answer all of them or a claim that you offer every service. Start with Children and work through the relevant pages when you have time. Mark topics you skip or want someone else to answer.

Each page keeps your interview notes, a summary, any corrections and questions still needing confirmation. Review the summary before marking a page reviewed. Michael will use it to draft website copy for you to check; nothing is published automatically.

When you are ready, ask the assistant to export a ZIP. Check its file list, then send that handback ZIP to Michael through your usual channel. It contains the index and page Markdown files only. Keep the original folder so you can continue later.

If your app cannot make the ZIP, manually zip only `00-index.md` and the `pages` folder inside `answers/support-page-interview`. Do not zip your whole computer folder or add patient documents.
