Before you book

Questions people often ask us

Still unsure? Give us a call. Pierre or Stelios is usually happy to talk it through.

(02) 9890 7111
Will an adjustment hurt?

Adjustments don't need to be forceful. We choose a technique to suit you, and many people are surprised by how light it feels. Some people notice mild soreness afterwards. Tell us how you're going at any point and we'll change our approach.

Do you crack backs?

Not necessarily. We often use gentle instrument-assisted adjustments, and only use manual techniques when they're appropriate and you're comfortable with them.

Do I need to get changed?

No. You stay fully clothed. You might just be asked to take off a heavy jumper or jacket.

Will I need X-rays?

Not always. After your first assessment we'll tell you whether imaging may be useful, explain why, and let you know about any cost before you decide.

What does it cost?

Our new patient fees are on the fees page. If care is recommended, we go through the cost of the plan at your second visit before anything begins.

Do I need a referral?

No referral needed. If you already have scans or reports, bring them along.
