How can we help you today?

I'm a private patient

Recovering from injury or surgery, or managing a long-term condition. Private health rebates and Medicare care plans accepted.

I'm on the NDIS

Self-managed, plan-managed or NDIA-managed. Physiotherapy, exercise physiology and hydrotherapy under Capacity Building.

Getting started with NDIS

I'm on an aged-care package

Support at Home, Home Care Packages or CHSP. Strength, balance and falls prevention at home or in the pool.

Our services

Physiotherapy, exercise physiology and hydrotherapy, delivered by the same team.

Physiotherapy

Hands-on treatment and targeted exercise to recover from injury, surgery or neurological change, and to restore movement.

  • Neurological physiotherapy
  • Aged care physiotherapy
  • Falls prevention and balance
  • Post-surgery and joint replacement
About physiotherapy

Exercise Physiology

Structured, clinically prescribed exercise for long-term conditions, disability, mental health and healthy ageing.

  • Active ageing programs
  • NDIS and aged care gym
  • Chronic disease management
  • Group classes
About exercise physiology

Hydrotherapy

Warm water exercise in the pools at the aquatic centres where our clinics are based. Easier on sore joints and good for building confidence.

  • Hydrotherapy for arthritis
  • Aqua classes for seniors
  • NDIS hydrotherapy
  • Find a pool near you
About hydrotherapy

Also available: remedial massage, acupuncture and dry needling. Our Hurstville clinic also offers cupping, gua sha and low-level laser therapy.
