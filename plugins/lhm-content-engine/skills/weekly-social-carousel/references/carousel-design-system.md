# LHM carousel design system

## Design direction

Use LHM's dark navy, orange, soft blue, and white system. The work may borrow the high-contrast editorial rhythm of strong marketing carousels, but it must not copy another creator's black theme, badge system, typography, or composition.

Core colours:

- navy 950: `#071625`
- navy 900: `#0B1C30`
- navy 800: `#122B49`
- orange: `#F58A47`
- soft blue: `#7FB3FF`
- white: `#F8FAFC`
- muted text: `#B7C3D4`

Use Avenir Next when available, then Avenir, Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif.

## Canvas and safe area

- Instagram portrait: 1080 x 1350 pixels
- keep essential copy at least 76 pixels from each edge
- retain generous negative space
- footer remains quiet and secondary

## Cover variants

### Typography

Use the navy gradient, one compact eyebrow, and one clear hook. Accent no more than one meaningful phrase.

### Image

Use a relevant, licensed, user-supplied, first-party product, or deliberately generated image. Copy the image into the package so the HTML remains portable. Add a dark navy overlay that protects contrast.

Good image subjects include a verified product interface, an analytics screen, a practice-owner workflow, or a specific visual metaphor. Avoid generic robots, glowing brains, hooded hackers, random code screens, and decorative stock imagery.

## Internal slides

Use one of the supported structures:

- `statement`: headline with optional short body
- `labels`: three or four short examples
- `checklist`: four to six scannable items
- `callout`: headline plus one bordered takeaway

Do not shrink text until a paragraph technically fits. Reduce the copy. A slide that needs text below the minimum type scale has failed editorial review.

## Review

Inspect the contact sheet at normal size. Then inspect the cover and the densest internal slide individually. Confirm no overflow, clipped copy, low-contrast text, broken image, accidental client identifier, or repeated footer collision.
